INNOVATION & TECHNOLOGY

The 3D Digital Fashion Engineering Atelier

Vidushi leads the vanguard of sustainable fashion by replacing wasteful physical sample cutting with high-fidelity 3D digital prototypes. Every garment is mathematically sculpted, draped in real-time, and tested under dynamic runway lighting before single shears touch the fabric.

  • ✦
    Interactive 360° Real-time Draping: Instant digital texture mapping onto 3D dress forms.
  • ✦
    Dynamic Cloth Physics & Breeze Simulation: Realistic fabric wave movement for runway testing.
  • ✦
    Paper Sketch Clean-up & Stencil Mapping: Seamless conversion from hand-drawn notebook sketches into 3D textiles.

04 / THE DESIGNER

About Vidushi

"Fashion is not merely about decorating the body — it is about engineering dreams, celebrating heritage, and commanding the room with effortless poise."

Vidushi is an emerging couture fashion designer recognized for her exquisite sensibility in balancing classic Indian craftsmanship with international red-carpet silhouettes. Her work challenges the boundaries between physical haute couture draping and futuristic digital fashion technology.

Trained in traditional textile arts, corsetry, and advanced 3D garment visualization, Vidushi creates bespoke bridal wear, evening ballgowns, and tailored architectural statements that empower the modern woman.

CORE COMPETENCIES & EXPERTISE

Haute Couture Draping (Moulage) 3D Garment CAD & Three.js Prototyping Architectural Corsetry & Boning Indian Zardozi & Aari Embroidery Pattern Drafting & Size Grading Production Tech Packs & Factory Specs Sustainable Silk & Velvet Sourcing Pantone Color Theory & Forecasting
