04 / The Designer

Meet Vidushi

Crafting Bespoke Couture with Heart, Grace & Precision

"Fashion is not simply about wearing a garment; it is about feeling untouchable, radiant, and truly yourself in every room you enter."

Every collection at Atelier Vidushi begins on pure white paper with hand-drawn graphite croquis. Drawing inspiration from royal Indian heritage architecture, floral motifs, and global runway tailoring, Vidushi personally curates the purest mulberry silks, rich velvets, and hand-woven organzas.

Whether it is a custom bridal lehenga, an architectural power blazer, or an ethereal red-carpet evening gown, each silhouette is tailored to the client's individual measurements, ensuring bespoke luxury that lasts a lifetime.
